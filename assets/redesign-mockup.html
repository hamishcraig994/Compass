What changes

Pill buttons, gradient washes, all-caps labels and "Top pick for you" eyebrows go. Buttons become 6px rectangles; posters get a 4px corner; chips leave the artwork.

Type

Sofia Sans Condensed for titles, the logo and numbers; Plus Jakarta Sans for the interface; Source Serif 4 for "because you watched" and "why Compass picked this". Bundled with the app, so nothing loads from Google.

Colour

Graphite screen and warm projector-white text. The theme accent appears only on the main action and the "why" rule, so the posters supply the colour. All six themes keep working, dark only.

Motion

Only the hero crossfade and responses to what you do. No lift-on-hover across every card; focus and hover use a light outline instead.
